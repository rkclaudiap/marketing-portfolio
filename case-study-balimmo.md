# Case Study — BALIMMO

## Context
BALIMMO is a Bali-based company operating across real estate, construction and villa management.

I joined as Marketing & Communications Director and built the marketing function from the ground up across multiple business divisions.

## Scope
- Built and structured a 10+ person multidisciplinary marketing team
- Defined workflows, responsibilities, production schedules and quality-control systems
- Managed content, creative, digital, partnerships and reporting
- Coordinated campaigns across Construction and Villa Management
- Developed CRM processes, customer journeys and centralised reporting

## Results
- Increased monthly content output from 121 to 519 deliverables (+329%)
- Generated 679K+ cross-platform impressions in one month
- Reached 700K+ Instagram views in 90 days
- 78% of Instagram views came from non-followers
- Directed 21+ villa productions
- Oversaw 6,300+ edited photos and 100+ video assets

## What I Built
- A structured multidisciplinary marketing department
- Cross-functional workflows and production systems
- A partnership programme targeting 21 influencers, artists, media and athletes
- A two-year CSR programme
- Investor presentations, brochures and sales collateral
- CRM and reporting systems connecting marketing and commercial teams

## Core Skills Used
Marketing Strategy • Team Leadership • Project Management • Partnerships • Content Operations • CRM • Reporting • Cross-functional Coordination
