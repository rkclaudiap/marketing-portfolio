# Case Study — Les Frenchies

## Context
Les Frenchies was an independent marketing and communications project supporting a group of 4 restaurants in Singapore.

The objective was to strengthen brand visibility, improve social media performance and support customer acquisition through content, digital marketing and partnerships.

## Scope
- Managed Instagram and Facebook strategy across multiple restaurant brands
- Built content calendars and campaign plans
- Coordinated photo and video production
- Managed publishing, community management and Meta Ads
- Developed giveaways, influencer collaborations and media partnerships
- Supported restaurant visibility and customer acquisition initiatives

## Results
- Delivered approximately 900 edited photos per month across the restaurant portfolio
- Grew Solo Ristorante’s Instagram audience from 4,700 to 9,000+ followers in 3 months (+91%)
- Increased brand visibility through targeted content and audience engagement
- Supported restaurant marketing through events, partnerships and digital campaigns

## What I Built
- Multi-brand social media strategy
- Content production workflow
- Audience growth strategy
- Influencer and media partnership approach
- Campaign and community management system
