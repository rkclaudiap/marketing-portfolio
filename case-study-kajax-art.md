# Case Study — Kajax Art / Cyril Kongo

## Context
Kajax Art represented French contemporary artist Cyril Kongo across Singapore, Bali and international markets.

I worked as General Manager, leading brand representation, event production, client relationships and operational delivery across premium private, corporate and cultural projects.

## Scope
- Led global brand representation and VIP experience across Asia and Europe
- Managed private and corporate event projects from concept through delivery
- Coordinated C-level stakeholders, venues, suppliers and production partners
- Developed proposals, negotiated terms and converted private and B2B opportunities
- Managed budgets, timelines, logistics and on-site execution
- Oversaw studio operations including artwork inventory, suppliers and space readiness
- Supported premium content production and documentary projects

## Results
- Delivered 8 major events of 200+ guests each
- Generated 400K+ SGD in event revenue, including supplier-related revenue
- Produced 200+ premium media assets and 1 documentary
- Managed projects involving H.S.H. Prince Albert II of Monaco and HM the King of Bhutan
- Delivered an activation within ART SG, an international platform welcoming 45,000+ visitors
- Worked with clients and partners including AXA, Mercer, Maison du Whisky and Rum Neisson
