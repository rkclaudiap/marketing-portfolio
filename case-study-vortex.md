# Case Study — Vortex Space & Media

## Context
Vortex Space & Media was a new multi-purpose business in Singapore combining coworking, private offices, events and media production.

I joined as General Manager and worked alongside the founder from pre-opening through launch, helping build the operational, commercial and brand foundations of the business.

## Scope
- Co-built the business from inception through opening
- Led pre-opening readiness across venue setup, suppliers, staffing, workflows and budgets
- Developed pricing, memberships, booking systems and customer journeys
- Coordinated vendors, partners and internal stakeholders across multiple workstreams
- Supported brand positioning, communication and website direction
- Built the Media Production division as a new business line
- Delivered branded and sponsored events

## Results
- Generated S$9K in venue rental revenue from the first 3 events before the official opening
- Delivered 10+ activations
- Welcomed 2,000+ guests across events and activities
- Produced 450+ content assets
- Launched 4 complementary business functions under one venue

## What I Built
- Pre-opening operational systems
- Commercial pricing and membership structure
- Booking and customer journey processes
- Supplier and vendor network
- Event operations and delivery processes
- Media Production division for hospitality, lifestyle and real estate clients

## Core Skills Used
Business Operations • Pre-opening • Project Management • Commercial Strategy • Client Experience • Vendor Management • Event Delivery • Partnerships • Process Design
